Investor Awareness

Common Warning Signs of Financial Scams

Learn to recognize typical tactics used to deceive retail investors.

Guaranteed High Returns

Promises of fixed daily or monthly gains (e.g. "30% profit guaranteed") are a serious warning sign. Market investments carry risk, and claims should be independently checked.

Artificial Urgency & Deadlines

Claims that "only 5 spots remain" or "offer expires in 10 minutes" are pressure tactics designed to force action before you can verify credentials.

Private Chat Groups

Some scam campaigns use private groups to promote "exclusive insider tips." A group invitation alone does not establish fraud; verify who is behind any offer.

Requests for Credentials or OTP

Legitimate investment advisers never ask for your Demat trading password, banking OTP, or UPI PIN. Never share authentication credentials.

Celebrity & Executive Deepfakes

Scammers use AI to clone voices and animate videos of well-known public figures and business leaders endorsing fake investment schemes.

The approach

Separate checks. Honest uncertainty.

01 / Independent modalities

Authenticity is not intent

Media authenticity and financial claims are evaluated separately. A synthetic signal alone does not prove a scam; genuine media does not prove an offer is safe.

02 / Backend-confirmed

No invented progress or fields

The interface displays reported stages and response fields. When a value is missing, it is shown as unavailable rather than estimated.

03 / Know the limits

A screening tool, not a verdict on truth

Compression, poor lighting, background noise, incomplete context, and model limitations can affect results. Independently verify important claims and credentials.

04 / APK static analysis

Apps are not run

APK checks inspect package information and permissions without executing the app.
